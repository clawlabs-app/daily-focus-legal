### 2. Description of the App

Daily Focus is a meditation and mindfulness application for Android devices. It provides:
* Timed focus sessions (30 seconds to 5 minutes) with audio accompaniment.
* Procedurally generated pattern-style artwork created from sensor data captured during your session.
* The ability to export your generated artwork via the Android share sheet (gallery and other supported apps).
* Aggregate real-time statistics showing the number of active users globally (no personal identification).
